Anharmonic Potential

The anharmonic potential operator is defined as:

\[\begin{split}\Vanharm \equiv& \frac{1}{3!} \sum_{ijk=1}^{\Nvib} g_{ijk}\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k +\\ & \frac{1}{4!} \sum_{ijkl=1}^{\Nvib} h_{ijkl}\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\qop_l\end{split}\]

where \(g_{ijk}\) and \(h_{ijkl}\) are the cubic and quartic force constants.

For convenience we break the cubic sum up into a term in which no index is repeated, a term in which one index is repeated, and a term in which three indices are repeated. Since the \(\qop_i\) commute and \(g_{ijk}\) is symmetric under permutation of its indices, this gives:

\[\begin{split}\frac{1}{3!}\sum_{ijk=1}^{\Nvib} g_{ijk}\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k =& \sum_{i<j<k}^{\Nvib} g_{ijk}\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\\ &+ \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i\neq j}^{\Nvib} g_{iij}\qop_i^2\qop_j\\ &+ \frac{1}{3!}\sum_{i=1}^{\Nvib} g_{iii}\qop_i^3\end{split}\]

Similarly, we break the quartic sum up into a term in which all four indices are distinct, a term in which exactly two indices are equal, a term with two distinct pairs of equal indices, a term in which exactly three indices are equal, and a term in which all four indices are equal. This gives:

\[\begin{split}\frac{1}{4!}\sum_{ijkl=1}^{\Nvib} h_{ijkl}\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\qop_l =& \sum_{i<j<k<l}^{\Nvib} h_{ijkl}\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\qop_l\\ &+ \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{\Nvib}\sum_{\substack{j<k\\ j,k\neq i}}^{\Nvib} h_{iijk}\qop_i^2\qop_j\qop_k\\ &+ \frac{1}{4}\sum_{i<j}^{\Nvib} h_{iijj}\qop_i^2\qop_j^2\\ &+ \frac{1}{3!}\sum_{i\neq j}^{\Nvib} h_{iiij}\qop_i^3\qop_j\\ &+ \frac{1}{4!}\sum_{i=1}^{\Nvib} h_{iiii}\qop_i^4\end{split}\]

For what it’s worth, the general form is:

\[\begin{split}\Vanharm =& \sum_{i\le j\le k}^{\Nvib} \frac{g_{ijk}}{\prod_{n=1}^{\Nvib} p_n!} \qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\\ &+ \sum_{i\le j\le k\le l}^{\Nvib} \frac{h_{ijkl}}{\prod_{n=1}^{\Nvib} p_n!} \qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\qop_l\end{split}\]

where \(p_n\) is the power of \(\qop_n\) in the term.

Matrix Elements

Because the operators on different modes commute, the matrix element of each term factors into one matrix element per mode (see Vibrational Wavefunction):

\[\begin{split}\braket{\vvec'\mid\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\mid\vvec} =& \prod_{n=1}^{\Nvib}\braket{v'_n\mid\qop_n^{p_n}\mid v_n}\\ \braket{\vvec'\mid\qop_i\qop_j\qop_k\qop_l\mid\vvec} =& \prod_{n=1}^{\Nvib}\braket{v'_n\mid\qop_n^{p_n}\mid v_n}\end{split}\]

For a mode that does not appear in the term, \(p_n=0\) and its factor is \(\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\). Collecting these factors into:

\[\delta^{(ij\ldots)}_{\vvec',\vvec} \equiv \prod_{n\notin\left\{i,j,\ldots\right\}}\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\]

and using the single-mode matrix elements \(\mathcal{Q}^{(p)}_{v'_n,v_n}\) from Vibrational Wavefunction, the matrix elements of \(\Vanharm\) are:

\[\begin{split}\braket{\vvec'\mid \Vanharm\mid\vvec} =& \sum_{i<j<k}^{\Nvib} g_{ijk}T^{(111)}_{ijk}\\ &+ \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i\neq j}^{\Nvib} g_{iij}T^{(210)}_{ij}\\ &+ \frac{1}{3!}\sum_{i=1}^{\Nvib} g_{iii}T^{(300)}_{i}\\ &+ \sum_{i<j<k<l}^{\Nvib} h_{ijkl}T^{(1111)}_{ijkl}\\ &+ \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i=1}^{\Nvib}\sum_{\substack{j<k\\ j,k\neq i}}^{\Nvib} h_{iijk}T^{(211)}_{ijk}\\ &+ \frac{1}{4}\sum_{i<j}^{\Nvib} h_{iijj}T^{(22)}_{ij}\\ &+ \frac{1}{3!}\sum_{i\neq j}^{\Nvib} h_{iiij}T^{(31)}_{ij}\\ &+ \frac{1}{4!}\sum_{i=1}^{\Nvib} h_{iiii}T^{(4)}_{i}\end{split}\]

where the tensors \(T^{(pqr\ldots)}_{ijk\ldots}\) are defined as:

\[\begin{split}T^{(111)}_{ijk} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}_{v'_i,v_i}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_j,v_j} \mathcal{Q}_{v'_k,v_k}\delta^{(ijk)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(210)}_{ij} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}^{(2)}_{v'_i,v_i}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_j,v_j} \delta^{(ij)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(300)}_{i} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}^{(3)}_{v'_i,v_i} \delta^{(i)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(1111)}_{ijkl} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}_{v'_i,v_i}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_j,v_j} \mathcal{Q}_{v'_k,v_k}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_l,v_l} \delta^{(ijkl)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(211)}_{ijk} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}^{(2)}_{v'_i,v_i}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_j,v_j} \mathcal{Q}_{v'_k,v_k} \delta^{(ijk)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(22)}_{ij} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}^{(2)}_{v'_i,v_i} \mathcal{Q}^{(2)}_{v'_j,v_j} \delta^{(ij)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(31)}_{ij} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}^{(3)}_{v'_i,v_i}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_j,v_j} \delta^{(ij)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\\ T^{(4)}_{i} \equiv& \mathcal{Q}^{(4)}_{v'_i,v_i}\delta^{(i)}_{\vvec',\vvec}\end{split}\]

In practice, the matrix elements of \(\Vanharm\) are computed by iterating over the \(\binom{\Nvib+2}{3}\) cubic and \(\binom{\Nvib+3}{4}\) quartic terms. Then for each term, the non-zero matrix elements are evaluated by using the selection rules for the relevant single-mode matrix elements.

In the full ansantz, the matrix elements of \(\Vanharm\) are diagonal in \(J\) and \(K\), so we can define a tensor:

\[\braket{\vvec', J', K' \mid \Vanharm\mid\vvec, J, K} = \delta_{J',J}\delta_{K',K} \braket{\vvec'\mid \Vanharm\mid\vvec}\]
