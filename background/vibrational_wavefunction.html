Vibrational Wavefunction

The point of this page is to derive relationships involving the vibrational component of the rovibrational wavefunction, \(\ket{\vvec}\). Here, \(\vvec\) is a vector of vibrational quantum numbers, one for each of the \(\Nvib\) vibrational modes of the molecule.

Matrix elements involving the vibrational wavefunciton are derived using second quantization. In mass-weighted normal coordinates:

\[\begin{split}\qop_n =& \sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}}\left(\aop^\dagger_n + \aop_n\right)\\ \pop_n =& i\sqrt{\frac{\hbar\omega_n}{2}}\left(\aop^\dagger_n - \aop_n\right)\end{split}\]

where \(\aop^\dagger_n\) and \(\aop_n\) are the creation and annihilation operators for the \(n\)-th vibrational mode, and \(\omega_n\) is the frequency of that mode. The action of these operators on the vibrational eigenstates are given by:

\[\begin{split}\aop^\dagger_n\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}} =& \sqrt{v_n+1}\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n+1,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}}\\ \aop_n\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}} =& \sqrt{v_n}\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n-1,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}}\end{split}\]

Thus the action of \(\qop_n\) and \(\pop_n\) on the vibrational eigenstates are:

\[\begin{split}\qop_n\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}} =& \sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}}\left[ \sqrt{v_n+1}\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n+1,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}} +\right.\\ &\left.\sqrt{v_n}\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n-1,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}}\right]\\ \pop_n\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}} =& i\sqrt{\frac{\hbar\omega_n}{2}}\left[ \sqrt{v_n+1}\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n+1,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}} -\right.\\ &\left.\sqrt{v_n}\ket{v_1,\ldots,v_n-1,\ldots,v_{\Nvib}}\right]\end{split}\]

Note that \(\qop_n\) and \(\pop_n\) obey the commutation relation:

\[\left[\qop_n,\pop_m\right] = i\hbar\delta_{nm}\]

Thus operators on different modes commute.

Matrix Elements

We will have to evaluate matrix elements of the form \(\braket{\vvec'\mid \op{O}\mid\vvec}\) where \(\op{O}\) is a product of \(\qop\) and \(\pop\) operators. Using the commutation relation for \(n\neq m\), the product of \(\qop\) and \(\pop\) operators factors into one matrix element per mode:

\[\braket{\vvec'\mid \op{O}_1 \op{O}_2\cdots \op{O}_p\mid\vvec} = \prod_{n=1}^{\Nvib}\braket{v'_n\mid \op{O}^{(n)}\mid v_n}\]

where \(\op{O}^{(n)}\) is the product, in the original order, of the operators that act on mode \(n\) (the identity if there are none) and we have used the fact that only \(\op{O}^{(n)}\) acts on mode \(n\).

In general, each \(\op{O}^{(n)}\) will be made up of products containing \(\qop_n\) and \(\pop_n\) operators. Assuming \(\op{O}^{(n)}\) is a product of \(p\) operators, we can invoke the resolution of the identity \(p-1\) times to factor the matrix element into a product of single-mode matrix elements:

\[\braket{v'_n\mid \op{O}^{(n)}\mid v_n} = \sum_{v^{(2)}_n\ldots v^{(p)}_n} \braket{v'_n\mid \op{O}^{(n)}_1\mid v^{(2)}_n} \braket{v^{(2)}_n\mid \op{O}^{(n)}_2 \mid v^{(3)}_n}\cdots \braket{v^{(p)}_n\mid \op{O}^{(n)}_{p}\mid v_n}\]

where \(\op{O}^{(n)}_i\) is the \(i\)-th operator in the product \(\op{O}^{(n)}\) and we have labeled the intermediate states with superscripts (as opposed to primes) for convenience. The single mode matrix elements of \(\qop_n\) and \(\pop_n\) are:

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_n,v_n} \equiv \braket{v'_n \mid\qop_n\mid v_n} =& \sqrt{\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}}\left[\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v'_n,v_n+1} + \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v'_n,v_n-1}\right]\\ \mathcal{P}_{v'_n,v_n} \equiv \braket{v'_n \mid\pop_n\mid v_n} =& i\sqrt{\frac{\hbar\omega_n}{2}}\left[\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v'_n,v_n+1} - \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v'_n,v_n-1}\right]\end{split}\]

The matrices \(\mathcal{Q}\) and \(\mathcal{P}\) are extremely sparse, and the matrix multiplication implied by an equation like:

\[\braket{v'_n\mid \op{Q}_n^2\mid v_n} = \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\mathcal{Q}_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n}\mathcal{Q}_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n}\]

is very inefficient. Instead we work out explicit formulas for the needed powers

Squared

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}^{(2)}_{v'_n,v_n}\equiv& \braket{v'_n\mid \op{Q}_n^2\mid v_n} = \frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}\sum_{v^{(2)}_n} \left[\sqrt{v^{(2)}_n+1}\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n+1} + \sqrt{v^{(2)}_n}\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n-1}\right]\\ & \left[\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} + \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1}\right]\end{split}\]

The four cross terms:

\[\begin{split}\sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v^{(2)}_n+1}\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n+1} \sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& \sqrt{(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v^{(2)}_n+1}\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n+1} \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& v_n\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v^{(2)}_n}\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n-1} \sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1}=& \left(v_n+1\right)\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v^{(2)}_n}\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n-1} \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1}=& \sqrt{\left(v_n-1\right)\left(v_n\right)}\delta_{v'_n+2,v_n}\end{split}\]

and the result:

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}^{(2)}_{v'_n,v_n} =& \frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}\left[ \sqrt{(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v_n}+ \left(2v_n+1\right)\delta_{v'_n,v_n}+\right.\\ &\left. \sqrt{\left(v_n-1\right)\left(v_n\right)}\delta_{v'_n+2,v_n} \right]\end{split}\]

Cubed

Inserting the resolution of the identity between \(\op{Q}_n^2\) and \(\op{Q}_n\), and using the squared result:

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}^{(3)}_{v'_n,v_n}\equiv& \braket{v'_n\mid \op{Q}_n^3\mid v_n} = \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\braket{v'_n\mid \op{Q}_n^2\mid v^{(2)}_n} \braket{v^{(2)}_n\mid \op{Q}_n\mid v_n}\\ =& \left(\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}\right)^{3/2}\sum_{v^{(2)}_n} \left[\sqrt{(v^{(2)}_n+2)(v^{(2)}_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v^{(2)}_n}+ \left(2v^{(2)}_n+1\right)\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n}+\right.\\ &\left.\sqrt{\left(v^{(2)}_n-1\right)\left(v^{(2)}_n\right)} \delta_{v'_n+2,v^{(2)}_n}\right] \left[\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} + \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1}\right]\end{split}\]

The six cross terms:

\[\begin{split}\sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{(v^{(2)}_n+2)(v^{(2)}_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& \sqrt{(v_n+3)(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-3,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{(v^{(2)}_n+2)(v^{(2)}_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& v_n\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v'_n-1,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\left(2v^{(2)}_n+1\right)\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& \left(2v_n+3\right)\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v'_n-1,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\left(2v^{(2)}_n+1\right)\delta_{v'_n,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& \left(2v_n-1\right)\sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v'_n+1,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{\left(v^{(2)}_n-1\right)\left(v^{(2)}_n\right)} \delta_{v'_n+2,v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& \left(v_n+1\right)\sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v'_n+1,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{\left(v^{(2)}_n-1\right)\left(v^{(2)}_n\right)} \delta_{v'_n+2,v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& \sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)\left(v_n-2\right)}\delta_{v'_n+3,v_n}\end{split}\]

The second and third terms both have \(\delta_{v'_n-1,v_n}\) and sum to \(3\left(v_n+1\right)^{3/2}\); the fourth and fifth terms both have \(\delta_{v'_n+1,v_n}\) and sum to \(3v_n^{3/2}\). The result is:

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}^{(3)}_{v'_n,v_n} =& \left(\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}\right)^{3/2}\left[ \sqrt{(v_n+3)(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-3,v_n}+ 3\left(v_n+1\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n-1,v_n}+\right.\\ &\left. 3v_n^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n+1,v_n}+ \sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)\left(v_n-2\right)}\delta_{v'_n+3,v_n} \right]\end{split}\]

Fourth Power

Inserting the resolution of the identity between \(\op{Q}_n^3\) and \(\op{Q}_n\), and using the cubed result:

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}^{(4)}_{v'_n,v_n}\equiv&\braket{v'_n\mid \op{Q}_n^4\mid v_n} = \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\braket{v'_n\mid \op{Q}_n^3\mid v^{(2)}_n} \braket{v^{(2)}_n\mid \op{Q}_n\mid v_n}\\ =& \left(\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}\right)^{2}\sum_{v^{(2)}_n} \left[\sqrt{(v^{(2)}_n+3)(v^{(2)}_n+2)(v^{(2)}_n+1)} \delta_{v'_n-3,v^{(2)}_n}+\right.\\ &3\left(v^{(2)}_n+1\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n-1,v^{(2)}_n}+ 3\left(v^{(2)}_n\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n+1,v^{(2)}_n}+\\ &\left.\sqrt{v^{(2)}_n\left(v^{(2)}_n-1\right)\left(v^{(2)}_n-2\right)} \delta_{v'_n+3,v^{(2)}_n}\right]\\ &\left[\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} + \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1}\right]\end{split}\]

The eight cross terms:

\[\begin{split}\sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{(v^{(2)}_n+3)(v^{(2)}_n+2)(v^{(2)}_n+1)} \delta_{v'_n-3,v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& \sqrt{(v_n+4)(v_n+3)(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-4,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{(v^{(2)}_n+3)(v^{(2)}_n+2)(v^{(2)}_n+1)} \delta_{v'_n-3,v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& v_n\sqrt{(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}3\left(v^{(2)}_n+1\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n-1,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& 3\left(v_n+2\right)\sqrt{(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}3\left(v^{(2)}_n+1\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n-1,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& 3v_n^2\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}3\left(v^{(2)}_n\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n+1,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& 3\left(v_n+1\right)^2\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n}3\left(v^{(2)}_n\right)^{3/2}\delta_{v'_n+1,v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& 3\left(v_n-1\right)\sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)}\delta_{v'_n+2,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v^{(2)}_n\left(v^{(2)}_n-1\right)\left(v^{(2)}_n-2\right)} \delta_{v'_n+3,v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v_n+1}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n+1} =& \left(v_n+1\right)\sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)}\delta_{v'_n+2,v_n}\\ \sum_{v^{(2)}_n} \sqrt{v^{(2)}_n\left(v^{(2)}_n-1\right)\left(v^{(2)}_n-2\right)} \delta_{v'_n+3,v^{(2)}_n}\sqrt{v_n}\delta_{v^{(2)}_n,v_n-1} =& \sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)\left(v_n-2\right)\left(v_n-3\right)} \delta_{v'_n+4,v_n}\end{split}\]

The second and third terms both have \(\delta_{v'_n-2,v_n}\) and sum to \(\left(4v_n+6\right)\sqrt{(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\); the fourth and fifth terms both have \(\delta_{v'_n,v_n}\) and sum to \(6v_n^2+6v_n+3\); the sixth and seventh terms both have \(\delta_{v'_n+2,v_n}\) and sum to \(\left(4v_n-2\right)\sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)}\). The result is:

\[\begin{split}\mathcal{Q}^{(4)}_{v'_n,v_n} =& \left(\frac{\hbar}{2\omega_n}\right)^{2}\left[ \sqrt{(v_n+4)(v_n+3)(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-4,v_n}+ \left(4v_n+6\right)\sqrt{(v_n+2)(v_n+1)}\delta_{v'_n-2,v_n}+\right.\\ &\left(6v_n^2+6v_n+3\right)\delta_{v'_n,v_n}+ \left(4v_n-2\right)\sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)}\delta_{v'_n+2,v_n}+\\ &\left. \sqrt{v_n\left(v_n-1\right)\left(v_n-2\right)\left(v_n-3\right)} \delta_{v'_n+4,v_n}\right]\end{split}\]
