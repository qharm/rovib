Watson mu Tensor

The problem description notes that the Watson mu tensor, \(\muvec\), is expanded as a fourth-order Taylor series about the minimum energy geometry, \(\muvec \approx \sum_{n=0}^{4}\muvec^{(n)}\). This page works out the details.

General Formulation

The \(n\)-th term in the Taylor expansion of \(\muvec\) is given by:

\[\muvec^{(n)} = c_n \Izmhalf \bvec^n \Izmhalf\]

with:

  • \(c_n = (n+1)\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^n\)

  • \(\bvec = \sum_{k=1}^{\Nvib} \Izmhalf \avec_k \Izmhalf \qop_k\)

and \(\avec_k\) is a 3 by 3 matrix with elements:

\[a_{k,\alpha\beta} = 2 \sum_{i=1}^{\Natom} \sum_{\gamma,\delta=1}^{3} \left(\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta_{\gamma\delta} - \delta_{\alpha\delta}\delta_{\beta\gamma}\right) \sqrt{M_i}\, r_{i,\gamma}^0 L_{i,k,\delta}\]

0-th Order Term

The 0-th order term is straightforward:

\[\begin{split}\muvec^{(0)} =& c_0 \Izmhalf \bvec^0 \Izmhalf\\ =& 1 \cdot \Izmhalf \Izmhalf\\ =& \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\end{split}\]

or in components:

\[\muop^{(0)}_{\alpha\beta} = \frac{\delta_{\alpha\beta}} {I^0_{\alpha\alpha}}\]

where we used the fact that \(\Izvec\) is diagonal.

1-st Order Term

\[\begin{split}\muvec^{(1)} =& c_1 \Izmhalf \bvec^1 \Izmhalf\\ =& - \Izmhalf \bvec \Izmhalf\\ =& - \sum_{k=1}^{\Nvib} \Izmhalf \left(\Izmhalf \avec_k \Izmhalf\right) \Izmhalf \qop_k\\ =& -\sum_{k=1}^{\Nvib} \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_k\left(\Izvec\right)^{-1} \qop_k\end{split}\]

or in components:

\[\begin{split}\muop^{(1)}_{\alpha\beta} =& -\sum_{k=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\gamma,\delta=1}^{3} \frac{\delta_{\alpha\gamma}}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}} a_{k,\gamma\delta} \frac{\delta_{\delta\beta}}{I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\\ =&-\sum_{k=1}^{\Nvib} \frac{1}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}I^0_{\beta\beta}} a_{k,\alpha\beta} \qop_k\\ =&-\sum_{k=1}^{\Nvib} \frac{\xi^k_{\alpha\beta}}{I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\end{split}\]

where we have defined:

\[\xi^k_{\alpha\beta} \equiv \frac{a_{k,\alpha\beta}}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}}\]

2-nd Order Term

\[\begin{split}\muvec^{(2)} =& c_2 \Izmhalf \bvec^2 \Izmhalf\\ =& \frac{3}{4} \Izmhalf \bvec^2 \Izmhalf\\ =& \frac{3}{4} \sum_{k,\ell=1}^{\Nvib} \Izmhalf \left(\Izmhalf \avec_k \Izmhalf\right) \left(\Izmhalf \avec_\ell \Izmhalf\right)\\ & \Izmhalf \qop_k\qop_\ell\\ =& \frac{3}{4} \sum_{k,\ell=1}^{\Nvib} \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_k \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_\ell \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1} \qop_k\qop_\ell\end{split}\]

or in components:

\[\begin{split}\muop^{(2)}_{\alpha\beta} =& \frac{3}{4}\sum_{k,\ell=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\gamma,\delta,\epsilon,\zeta=1}^{3} \frac{\delta_{\alpha\gamma}}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}} a_{k,\gamma\delta} \frac{\delta_{\delta\epsilon}}{I^0_{\delta\delta}} a_{\ell,\epsilon\zeta} \frac{\delta_{\zeta\beta}}{I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\qop_\ell\\ =& \frac{3}{4}\sum_{k,\ell=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta=1}^{3} \frac{1}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}} a_{k,\alpha\delta} \frac{1}{I^0_{\delta\delta}} a_{\ell,\delta\beta} \frac{1}{I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\qop_\ell\\ =& \frac{3}{4}\sum_{k,\ell=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta=1}^{3} \frac{1}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}I^0_{\delta\delta}I^0_{\beta\beta}} a_{k,\alpha\delta} a_{\ell,\delta\beta} \qop_k\qop_\ell\\ =& \frac{3}{4}\sum_{k,\ell=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta=1}^{3} \frac{\xi^k_{\alpha\delta}\xi^\ell_{\delta\beta}} {I^0_{\beta\beta}}\qop_k\qop_\ell\end{split}\]

3-rd Order Term

\[\begin{split}\muvec^{(3)} =& c_3 \Izmhalf \bvec^3 \Izmhalf\\ =& -\frac{1}{2} \Izmhalf \bvec^3 \Izmhalf\\ =& -\frac{1}{2} \sum_{k,\ell,m=1}^{\Nvib} \Izmhalf \left(\Izmhalf \avec_k \Izmhalf\right) \left(\Izmhalf \avec_\ell \Izmhalf\right)\\ & \left(\Izmhalf \avec_m \Izmhalf\right) \Izmhalf \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\\ =& -\frac{1}{2} \sum_{k,\ell,m=1}^{\Nvib} \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_k \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_\ell \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_m \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\end{split}\]

and in components:

\[\begin{split}\muop^{(3)}_{\alpha\beta} =& -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k,\ell,m=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\gamma,\delta,\epsilon,\zeta,\theta,\iota=1}^{3} \frac{\delta_{\alpha\gamma}}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}} a_{k,\gamma\delta} \frac{\delta_{\delta\epsilon}}{I^0_{\delta\delta}} a_{\ell,\epsilon\zeta} \frac{\delta_{\zeta\theta}}{I^0_{\zeta\zeta}} a_{m,\theta\iota} \frac{\delta_{\iota\beta}}{I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\\ =& -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k,\ell,m=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta,\zeta=1}^{3} \frac{1}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}I^0_{\delta\delta} I^0_{\zeta\zeta}I^0_{\beta\beta}} a_{k,\alpha\delta} a_{\ell,\delta\zeta} a_{m,\zeta\beta} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\\ =& -\frac{1}{2}\sum_{k,\ell,m=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta,\zeta=1}^{3} \frac{\xi^k_{\alpha\delta}\xi^\ell_{\delta\zeta}\xi^m_{\zeta\beta}} {I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\end{split}\]

4-th Order Term

\[\begin{split}\muvec^{(4)} =& c_4 \Izmhalf \bvec^4 \Izmhalf\\ =& -\frac{5}{8} \Izmhalf \bvec^4 \Izmhalf\\ =& -\frac{5}{8} \sum_{k,\ell,m,p=1}^{\Nvib} \Izmhalf \left(\Izmhalf \avec_k \Izmhalf\right) \left(\Izmhalf \avec_\ell \Izmhalf\right)\\ & \left(\Izmhalf \avec_m \Izmhalf\right) \left(\Izmhalf \avec_p \Izmhalf\right) \Izmhalf \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\qop_p\\ =& -\frac{5}{8} \sum_{k,\ell,m,p=1}^{\Nvib} \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_k \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_\ell \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_m \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1}\avec_p \left(\Izvec\right)^{-1} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\qop_p\end{split}\]

and in components:

\[\begin{split}\muop^{(4)}_{\alpha\beta} =& -\frac{5}{8}\sum_{k,\ell,m,p=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\gamma,\delta,\epsilon,\zeta,\theta,\iota,\kappa,\lambda=1}^{3} \frac{\delta_{\alpha\gamma}}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}} a_{k,\gamma\delta} \frac{\delta_{\delta\epsilon}}{I^0_{\delta\delta}} a_{\ell,\epsilon\zeta} \frac{\delta_{\zeta\theta}}{I^0_{\zeta\zeta}} a_{m,\theta\iota} \frac{\delta_{\iota\kappa}}{I^0_{\iota\iota}} a_{p,\kappa\lambda} \frac{\delta_{\lambda\beta}}{I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\qop_p\\ =& -\frac{5}{8}\sum_{k,\ell,m,p=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta,\zeta,\iota=1}^{3} \frac{1}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}I^0_{\delta\delta}I^0_{\zeta\zeta} I^0_{\iota\iota}I^0_{\beta\beta}} a_{k,\alpha\delta} a_{\ell,\delta\zeta} a_{m,\zeta\iota} a_{p,\iota\beta} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\qop_p\\ =& -\frac{5}{8}\sum_{k,\ell,m,p=1}^{\Nvib} \sum_{\delta,\zeta,\iota=1}^{3} \frac{\xi^k_{\alpha\delta}\xi^\ell_{\delta\zeta} \xi^m_{\zeta\iota}\xi^p_{\iota\beta}} {I^0_{\beta\beta}} \qop_k\qop_\ell\qop_m\qop_p\end{split}\]

n-th Order Term

For the \(n\)th order term it is helpful to define a multi-index \(\midx_n = (i_1,i_2,\ldots,i_n)\) with \(i_j \in [1,\Nvib]\). We can then define the tensor \(\tens{\eta}\) as:

\[\eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta} \equiv c_n\sum_{\delta_1,\delta_2,\ldots,\delta_{n-1}=1}^{3} \frac{\xi^{i_1}_{\alpha\delta_1}\xi^{i_2}_{\delta_1\delta_2} \xi^{i_3}_{\delta_2\delta_3}\cdots \xi^{i_n}_{\delta_{n-1}\beta}} {I^0_{\beta\beta}}\]

where the \(\xi\) on the right-side are our previously defined 1-st order terms. Also defining \(\qop_{\midx_n} \equiv \qop_{i_1}\qop_{i_2}\cdots\qop_{i_n}\), for \(n \geq 1\) we can write the \(n\)th order term as:

\[\muop^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta} = \sum_{\midx_n} \eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta}\qop_{\midx_n}\]

For \(n=0\) the multi-index is empty, so the sum has a single term. Taking \(\qop_{\midx_0} \equiv 1\) and

\[\eta^{\midx_0}_{\alpha\beta} \equiv \frac{\delta_{\alpha\beta}}{I^0_{\alpha\alpha}}\]

the same expression reproduces the 0-th order term derived above, so it holds for all \(n \geq 0\).
