Rovibrational Coupling Hamiltonian

The rovibrational coupling Hamiltonian is defined as:

\[\Hrovib \equiv \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{4}\jvec^T\muvec^{(n)}\jvec - \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=0}^{3}\left(\jvec^T\muvec^{(n)}\pivec + \pivec^T\muvec^{(n)}\jvec\right)\]

Since \(\jvec\) is purely rotational it commutes with \(\qvec\) and \(\pvec\), and thus with \(\muvec^{(n)}\) and \(\pivec\). \(\muvec^{(n)}\) is also symmetric (the individual \(\eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta}\) are not, but reversing the multi-index transposes them and the normal coordinates commute). Relabeling \(\alpha\leftrightarrow\beta\) then gives:

\[\pivec^T\muvec^{(n)}\jvec = \sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{3}\piop_\alpha\muop^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta}\jop_\beta = \sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{3}\jop_\alpha\piop_\beta\muop^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta}\]

and therefore:

\[\Hrovib = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=1}^{4}\jvec^T\muvec^{(n)}\jvec - \frac{1}{2}\sum_{n=0}^{3}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{3}\jop_\alpha\left( \muop^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta}\piop_\beta + \piop_\beta\muop^{(n)}_{\alpha\beta}\right)\]

Writing out the matrix products and inserting the Taylor expansion of \(\muvec\):

\[\begin{split}\Hrovib =& \frac{1}{2}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{3} \jop_\alpha\jop_\beta \sum_{n=1}^{4}\sum_{\midx_n} \eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta}\qop_{\midx_n} -\\ &\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{3} \jop_\alpha \sum_{n=0}^{3}\sum_{\midx_n}\eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta} \sum_{i,j} \zeta^\beta_{ij}\left( \qop_{\midx_n}\qop_{i}\pop_{j} + \qop_{i}\pop_{j}\qop_{\midx_n}\right)\end{split}\]

where we have used the definition of \(\muvec^{(n)}\) from Watson mu Tensor.

Matrix Elements

\[\begin{split}\braket{\vvec',J',K'\mid \Hrovib\mid\vvec,J,K}=& \frac{1}{2}\sum_{\alpha,\beta=1}^{3} \braket{J',K'\mid\jop_\alpha\jop_\beta\mid J,K} T_{\alpha\beta}^{(\text{RC},0)} -\\ &\frac{1}{2}\sum_{\alpha=1}^{3} \braket{J',K'\mid\jop_\alpha\mid J,K} T_{\alpha}^{(\text{RC},1)}\\ T_{\alpha\beta}^{(\text{RC},0)}\equiv& \sum_{n=1}^{4}\sum_{\midx_n} \eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta} \braket{\vvec'\mid\qop_{\midx_n}\mid\vvec}\\ T_{\alpha}^{(\text{RC},1)}\equiv& \sum_{\beta=1}^{3}\sum_{n=0}^{3}\sum_{\midx_n}\eta^{\midx_n}_{\alpha\beta} \sum_{i,j} \zeta^\beta_{ij} \left( \braket{\vvec'\mid\qop_{\midx_n}\qop_{i}\pop_{j}\mid\vvec} +\right.\\ &\left. \braket{\vvec'\mid\qop_{i}\pop_{j}\qop_{\midx_n}\mid\vvec}\right)\end{split}\]
